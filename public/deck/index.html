01 / The objective

Private incentives.
Public benefit.

Expertise does not ensure public benefit. Participation can reveal needs and evidence, but cannot guarantee sound judgment.

01 / JUDGMENT

Draw on expertise.

Give difficult assessments the attention and knowledge they need. Expertise matters; headcount alone is no guarantee of quality.

02 / PUBLIC VOICE

Keep needs visible.

People affected can surface neglected needs, evidence and costs. Their participation can improve the judgment itself.

03 / OUR AIM

Reward useful work.

Make assessment and correction worthwhile. Test whether those rewards improve decisions and serve affected people.

Credible consensus: evidence-informed judgment under an explicit policy, with usable correction paths. An intermediate objective; public benefit is the outcome to test.

Framing: John Dewey (1927), pp. 206–209. The revised manuscript develops this incentive-alignment thesis. [1]

10 / Conditions for success

Contracts can enforce rules.
They cannot certify good judgment.

A defensible public purpose

A clear policy can still serve the wrong interests. Agreement under it does not establish public benefit.

Contestable evidence

Someone must find the problem, gather counter-evidence and pay to challenge it. The external arbitrator must resolve the dispute.

Informed, independent judgment

Stake does not certify expertise. Concentrated stake, coordination and shared bias can move the jury’s reference.

Explicit stop authority

A configured temporary circuit breaker lets its holder permanently stop future accrual once, before its authority expires.

The holder cannot take prior accrual. No general guarantee of truthful reporting or resistance to capture is established. [1, 2]

13 / Workshop arithmetic

The room sets the centre and the band.

Drag a score or use its arrow keys. Watch the mean, spread and band change together.

Centre: the stake-weighted mean. Here each seat has equal weight.

Band: weighted standard deviation sets its width. At essentially zero spread, distance forfeits switch off.

Points: closer scores inside the band share more of the pot; outside it, distance increases the forfeit.

The full implementation uses a different grading rule: final-jury median and median absolute deviation. See the comparison.

    Coherence can reward a shared mistake.

    Did the policy or the group become the anchor?

    What did the assigned role change?

    17 / Arrow’s role

    Arrow is a precursor.
    The full system is the destination.

    The proposed experiment

    Invited experts score a real funding docket under a published policy.

    Trusted nominations. A fixed round. Honesty, competence and non-collusion are assumptions.

    What this can tell us

    Can people use the policy?

    Study comprehension, reasons for disagreement, score stability and the practical experience of committing and revealing.

    What it cannot establish

    Permissionless selection, resistance to bribery or capture, accuracy adjudication, the appeal economy, or long-running funding safety.

    Experiment scope: proposal updated 9 October 2026. These are learning questions, not reported results. [3]

    19 / The takeaway

    Credible consensus.
    In service of public benefit.

    Reward informed assessment and correction to build credible consensus. Here, those judgments guide funding. Test whether each application serves its intended purpose. Agreement and participant profits alone do not establish public benefit.

    Appendix B / Score versus settlement

    One number funds projects.
    Another reference grades curators.

    Current full implementation

    Adopted score

    The deepest completed jury’s stake-weighted mean.

    Grading reference

    That jury’s weighted median and median absolute deviation (MAD), with a configured floor.

    Reports from all rounds are graded against this final band. Eligible reports share rewards; distance outside the band increases stake loss.

    Workshop simplification

    Shared mean and spread

    The stake-weighted mean and weighted standard deviation set the workshop centre and band.

    Immediate points

    Closeness changes reward shares; essentially zero spread disables distance forfeits.

    The workshop slider does not simulate current contract settlement. Its mean-based band and points differ from the implementation’s grading and rewards. [2]
