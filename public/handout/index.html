Informed judgment, public purpose

Reward assessment and correction to support better funding decisions. Credible consensus means evidence-informed judgments under an explicit policy, with usable correction paths. It is an intermediate objective, not certified truth. Public benefit needs separate evidence about outcomes for affected people.

The full mechanism

  1. Set terms: funding budget, public policy and fixed economic rules.
  2. Nominate: identify a beneficiary, publish evidence, request a weight and deposit the funding asset.
  3. Judge relevance: stake-backed, randomly drawn juries seal and reveal scores under the policy.
  4. Appeal: fund a larger jury. The deepest completed round supplies the score and the reference for grading every round.
  5. Fund: eligible weights split the continuing flow after any required holdback.
  6. Challenge and revisit: once scored, claims can face external arbitration when no relevance case is open. Re-scoring can change future funding.

Weight is not a funding share

Effective weight = the lower of requested weight and curated score. Funding shares depend on relative weights. Try the NumPy worked example: change a jury score and watch all funding shares update. Its evidence is real; the funding values are illustrative.

Dependencies and authority

Good outcomes need a defensible policy, informed jurors, usable evidence and affordable challenges. Stake does not certify expertise; agreement does not establish public benefit.

Where configured, a temporary circuit-breaker holder may permanently stop future accrual once, before expiry. They cannot take prior accrual.

Today: experience one layer

Read the policy, score alone, seal, then reveal. This hosted game uses equal seats and points. Its band follows the mean and standard deviation; rewards depend on closeness. At essentially zero spread, distance forfeits switch off.

Keep score proofs private. This game’s reporting bounty is absent from the current full-system contracts. The live jury game does not run accuracy disputes, jury selection, appeals or a funding stream.
