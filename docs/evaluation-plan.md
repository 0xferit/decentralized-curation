# Evaluation plan and limits

This is the proposed evidence plan for claims made in the presentation. It is not a report of a completed study, an approved protocol for the Arrow experiment, or a substitute for inspecting that experiment's full methods. The workshop teaches the arithmetic and exposes questions; assigned roles, shared discussion, points and a selected docket do not establish voluntary behavior or real-world efficacy.

The [public mechanism summary](../public/mechanism/index.html) distinguishes the intended design, the hosted exercise and implementation evidence. The [source register](presentation-sources.md) records the supporting research and version boundaries. The pool's stated policy supplies its decision objective; there is no protocol-wide definition or universal numerical scale of public benefit.

## Separate the claims before measuring them

| Claim to test | Independent measurement | Feasible comparison | What would lower confidence or require redesign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Participants understand the policy and payment rules | Before play, score explanations and unseen cases against a declared answer key for procedural facts; review policy reasoning against independently written criteria, allowing defensible disagreement | Same instruction time with ordinary written guidance, or a revised interface, on matched unseen cases | Persistent procedural errors, interpretations incompatible with the stated policy, or confidence rising without comprehension require clearer prompts or training before attributing behavior to incentives |
| Scores are repeatable when the information is unchanged | Repeat equivalent cases without feedback, with fresh order and recorded evidence access; report changes, uncertainty and systematic directional drift | A simple independent rating process with the same evidence, time and participant pool | Repeatability worse than the comparison, or stability achieved by ignoring relevant evidence, undermines the reliability claim; stability alone does not establish validity |
| New evidence improves judgments | Use predeclared cases with verified, decision-relevant changes; independently assess whether reasoning recognizes the change and applies the pool's policy | Separate groups receiving the original or updated evidence, or counterbalanced matched cases that avoid revealing answers | Reports that remain anchored despite decisive evidence, or move with irrelevant cues, require revising evidence presentation or incentives |
| Private rewards improve assessment rather than only agreement | Blind assessment of factual support, scope, counterarguments and policy fidelity in submitted reasons; separately record payoff and agreement | Independently chosen strategies under the proposed reward rule versus fixed compensation, with equal task, evidence and time | More agreement or profit without better independently assessed work does not support the claim; worse evidence use or excluded defensible dissent requires redesign |
| Challenges and appeals correct errors at usable cost | Independently audit samples of challenged and unchallenged items; measure corrections, upheld false claims, wrongful reversals, access, time, capital lockup and net cost by participant group | A stated feasible review process applied to comparable items and available resources | Errors persisting because nobody can afford to investigate or appeal, or corrections outweighed by wrongful reversals and costs, undermine the correction claim |
| Funded decisions serve the pool's declared purpose | Before allocation, affected people and accountable funders specify the target population, observable consequences and evaluation horizon; assess delivery, distribution of benefits and harms, and total process costs independently of jurors | A feasible allocation alternative with the same budget and opportunity set, such as an ordinary panel or eligible lottery where appropriate | No meaningful advantage over the alternative after costs, adverse distributional effects, or failure on the declared purpose requires narrowing the claim or changing the policy/process |
| The integrated protocol implements its stated accounting | Execute actual state transitions against explicit invariants: conservation, authorization, timing, reserves, retries, finality, migration and failure recovery | Reference accounting and adversarial executions of the same public semantics | Any unauthorized transfer, double payment, released liability or inconsistent final state blocks deployment; a document or simulation cannot substitute for this check |

For judgments without an objective answer key, independent review is a fallible comparison, not ground truth. Reviewers must be independent of the rewarded jury, blinded to its payments and experimental condition where feasible, and selected for relevant competence and representation of affected people. Publish their disagreement, conflicts, criteria and uncertainty. Do not score a report as high quality merely because it matches the group mean or eventual appeal outcome.

## Design the study before collecting confirmatory outcomes

Choose one primary question per study. Record the exact mechanism version, pool policy, evidence package, target and sampled populations, exclusions, recruitment, compensation, comparison, primary outcome, evaluation horizon and analysis before observing outcome data. Numerical success margins and sample size must come from the decision's practical stakes, expected uncertainty and pilot estimates; none has been justified for this presentation. An underpowered or imprecise result is inconclusive, not evidence of equality.

Jury members interact and their payouts depend on each other. When comparing incentive rules, assign complete independent groups or sessions to conditions and analyze at that level; do not count every seat or repeated report as an independent replication. Hold the task, evidence, time, recruitment and non-treatment compensation comparable. Record dependence through shared organizations, sources and prior contact rather than assuming separate wallets imply independent information. If group assignment or realistic incentives are infeasible, label the study observational or exploratory and limit causal claims accordingly.

Do not script adversarial behavior in the study used to estimate whether people choose it voluntarily. Assigned-role exercises can test operational defenses and calculate consequences under specified reports. An incentive study instead lets participants choose strategies under disclosed rules, records the choices, and uses an appropriate comparison. Any deception or undisclosed adverse consequence requires its own ethical review; this plan authorizes neither.

Measure investigation and opportunity costs, failed attempts, nonparticipation, attrition, unresolved cases and delayed payments. Audit unchallenged items so detection is not inferred only from disputes that occurred. Report all prespecified outcomes and deviations, including unfavorable results. Distinguish no observed attack from an estimated bound based on actual opportunity and exposure; neither proves an absence of profitable deviations.

## Workshop-specific discipline

Keep any directional forecast in a dated private facilitator note until every compared round is closed. Afterwards reveal the original wording, including failed predictions. This is not a registered experiment unless registration actually occurred. If participants learned the forecast before scoring, record that exposure.

Read a band's width alongside its mean, response count, recusals and reasons. A bounded score scale limits possible spread near its endpoints. Different nominees, feedback between rounds and shared participants also prevent the six rounds from serving as independent comparisons of the mechanism. The assigned adversarial role measures compliance and resulting payoffs in that scenario, not the population's willingness to cheat for profit.

The workshop's public results and reflections may motivate the studies above. Reusing participants' identifiable data for research requires a separately disclosed purpose and consent; the participation notice and session retention policy do not automatically authorize it.
