Who judges what matters?

Research depends on judgments about what to read, cite, reuse and reward. This proposal uses private incentives for assessment and correction to pursue credible consensus. Public-goods funding is one application.

The funding application

  1. Set terms: funding budget, public policy and fixed economic rules.
  2. Nominate: identify a beneficiary, publish evidence, request a weight and post a deposit. Claims need no prior truth approval.
  3. Judge relevance: stake-backed, randomly drawn juries seal and reveal scores under the policy.
  4. Appeal: publicly crowdfund competing scores. An opposed appeal doubles the jury target; payouts depend on distance to the final score.
  5. Fund: eligible weights split the continuing flow after any required holdback.
  6. Fact-check: after scoring, anyone can post a bond and fees to contest false or non-falsifiable claims, if no relevance case is open. An external arbitrator decides. A successful challenger takes the nomination’s bond and ends its pool benefits. The author may nominate again.
  7. Revisit: new relevance assessments can change future funding.

Weight is not a funding share

Effective weight = the lower of requested weight and curated score. These are funding ratios, not measured ratios of public benefit. A sole eligible positive weight takes the full flow. Jury fees come first; contest funds cover shortfalls. See the illustrative NumPy example.

A configured, temporary circuit-breaker holder can permanently stop future accrual once, before expiry, but cannot take prior accrual.

Today: experience one layer

Read the policy, score alone, seal, then reveal. This hosted game uses equal seats and points. Its band follows the mean and standard deviation; rewards depend on closeness. At essentially zero spread, distance forfeits switch off.

Use an alias or observe. Revealed scores and standings are visible; adversarial role-play may be assigned. Ask before photographing results. The host wipes session results afterward; they have no automatic expiry.

Keep score proofs private. This game’s reporting bounty is absent from the inspected contracts. The game does not run accuracy disputes, jury selection, appeals or a funding stream.
